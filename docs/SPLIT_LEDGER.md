# Split ledger

Every read of each OSWorld split by this project: online runs, offline analyses that selected or evaluated on its
episodes, and the decision each read fed. Written 2026-11-09 for the exact re-analysis (Z1). A read is listed even when
it fed no reported number (demos, broken runs). Each row cites a file of this repository as `path:line`; evidence
that exists only on the project's server or in the development repository is marked OUTSIDE or "(server)".

Short names: J = [docs/lab_notebook/JOURNAL.md](lab_notebook/JOURNAL.md), L = [docs/lab_notebook/LOG.md](lab_notebook/LOG.md),
RS = [docs/RESEARCH_SYSTEM.md](RESEARCH_SYSTEM.md). Times are UTC, dates 2026-11-07 to 2026-11-09. The logs are mostly
in French. Rows are ordered by split, then roughly by time.

## Summary
- **DIAG** (rows D1-D30): two diagnostic passes and two extra passes of the original, then every construction,
  screen, probe and judge study. 75 of its tasks were open to construction; the other 45 form the reserve.
- **Reserve** (rows R1-R23): meant to be read once, to confirm a committed candidate; no candidate was admitted. It was
  read by five non-admitted arms online (ExPO, the answer output-row edit, the corrective prompt, the merge, the
  closed-form edit), three runs of the original, an invalid run and a broken start, a branching pilot, and the merge's
  decision rule was fixed after its reserve scores were seen (R19). Its tasks also enter offline studies through the
  DIAG passes: 23 of the 268 episodes of the judge comparison and of Guard v2 (R5), and 3 of the 12 replayed episodes of
  the repair and escalation studies (R6). The paper discloses the online arms and the direction analysis
  (paper/sec/limits.tex, traps.tex:25), not R5, R6 or R13.
- **TEST** (rows T1-T16): five valid model runs (original on the pod, ExPO, a partial corrective-prompt run, the merge,
  the original on Modal), one invalid run, the guarded agent, offline reads of its outcomes (attribution, constraint
  drop, the escalation prediction, the placebo power simulation, a red-team review) and 11 live demonstration episodes
  on three tasks (T14, server only). The registered attribution study (3 arms x 3 seeds, plus a placebo arm) has not
  run any episode; when it runs it reads TEST again.
- **Unsplit** (rows U1-U4): 6 tasks run in infrastructure pilots on 2026-11-07; the other 115 were read by no run.
