# Frozen protocol before screening
Only completed original-model diag rollouts of seeds 0–5 form development data. Existing direction is not used. Fit diff-of-means per layer on t>=2 loop+/loop-. All outputs in /workspace/astra. No gradient, optimizer or training. One own GPU process enforced by flock; other sessions untouched.
Reference direction U10 for circuit decomposition; examine local directions too. Baseline screening subset deterministically selected with RNG 20261107, at most two steps per rollout per category. Use all available final seed6–7 steps with labels loop+/loop- plus all steps of successful rollouts, after freezing final patch. No seed50+/100+/300+ data read.
Each replay batch counts one pass BEFORE execution, including baseline, failed passes and final evaluation; 50 total maximum. Record every context. Rebuild exactly with holo.replay; score raw generated response plus EOS. Primary outcome is mean token log probability, averaged over steps; also sum log probability and task/rollout counts. Negative and success groups may overlap.
Development selection: seek loop+ delta <= -0.10 nat/token, and loop- AND success delta >= -0.02 nat/token, then minimize edited entries; these thresholds define an operational offline screen, not proof of behavior change. If none qualifies, report failure and best observed tradeoff, never call it fixed. At most three adaptive screening rounds; stop after two consecutive rounds without improvement in best loop+ decrease under preservation limits, or a newly qualifying smaller support. No tuning after examining held-out data. Final compare original, chosen patch and seeded Gaussian random direction with same layers/writers/topk/alpha. Report actual BF16 changes and perturbation norm, not just targeted count. Offline success needs heldout preservation and loop decrease by same thresholds and stronger loop decrease than random; no rollout efficacy claim.

Third-round diagnostic, decided before its completed results: an additional original/shared-U10 comparison erases the SAME reference direction in all writers/layers, and measures last-prefill projections and log-probability. This is a mechanistic upper bound, excluded from final candidate selection because it is deliberately global. These two replay batches count against the same 50-pass cap. No further adaptive round follows.
