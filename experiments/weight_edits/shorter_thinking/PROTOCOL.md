# astra-short: frozen protocol, 2026-11-07

Owner astra-short. Deadline 2026-11-08 08:00 UTC. Maximum 25 charged lots, including failed/partial jobs; a lot is one fixed setting x fixed panel x four seeds plus three T=0 text controls. Count requests and context completions separately. No gradients, optimization/training, API logit bias or generation-time stopping rule. Only a fixed untied output row delta.

Hypothesis: globally raise </think> logit and reduce unnecessary reasoning without degrading next actions. Global mean direction: mu = mean of 356 already captured original-model final normalized hidden states on six known TRAIN contexts (first25 tokens plus two healthy continuations). delta = dose * mu/(mu dot mu), token 248069. No Wait edit, no answer edit. Doses initially 2,4,8,12 logits on average; fixed before generation. One bounded adjustment sweep up to three interpolated doses if measurable length changes bracket desired reduction. Stop after two settings with no measurable length change (<2%), after no safe reduction in two sweeps, at deadline or at 25 lots. At most one process, <15GB, gpu_slot.sh, torch threads2.

Panel frozen before behavior: 28 construction contexts from six domains, fs+5/fs-5/healthy18, including 13 added middle steps from existing TRAIN allowlist tasks (successful and failed episodes). Nine task-disjoint heldout contexts reserved locally and never used for vector/dose design. Source train allowlist was pre-vetted by astra-mit1 against 45 confirmation tasks; no confirmation or test file read. Existing MIT1 training uses some heldout-ish names elsewhere; these nine are local heldout only, not official test.

Pilot: baseline and dose8 on three fixed contexts, one per label, to verify runtime then full baseline and 3-4 full doses k=4. Exclude pilot from full-panel paired metrics. Target mean thinking reduction 25/50/75%; admission requires >=25% on full panel, no JSON-validity drop, healthy semantic relevance loss<=5 percentage points, true-finish loss<=10 points, and three T=0 prompts semantically preserved. Measure P(answer) separately fs+ and fs-. Candidate may be sent to ares for online exploration under user's shortened-reasoning gate; this is NOT proof of false-success repair. That stronger claim additionally requires heldout false-answer reduction and independently audited useful continuation (historical causal gate -25pp and -50% relative).

Independent auditor judges screenshots+task+actions with arm names hidden where possible. All outputs retained including invalid/truncated. Agreement with logged action is a secondary proxy, not correctness. Original and edited regenerated from same inputs and seeds. Tie output embeddings explicitly undone. If admitted, freeze spec+delta hashes before heldout or sending. Paired random direction of equal row norm required before strong mechanistic claim; no causal success claimed from mere shortened text. Online score remains ares's independent validation.

Primary source: Cuadron et al. 2025 https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08235 (SWE-bench evidence, not direct Holo/OSWorld evidence).
